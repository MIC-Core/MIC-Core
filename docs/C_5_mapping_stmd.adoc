=== Purpose 

==== Administrative Data

[cols="1>m,2^e,7<",width="70%",align="center",frame="topbot",options="header"]
|===
| MIC Sim. Task Name| STMD Mapping | Obligation | Short Explanation
| Specific Product Name | administrative.specific-product.name | Mandatory | The Specific product name is the unique master name of the specific product
| Ordering Project Name |administrative.ordering-project.name | Mandatory | The Ordering project name is the unique master name of the ordering development project 
| Simulation request name | administrative.simulation-request.name | Mandatory | The Simulation request name is the unique name of the simulation request
| Simulation task name | administrative.simulation-task.name | Mandatory | The Simulation task name is the unique name of the simulation task
| Overall product name | administrative.overall-product.name | Recommended | The Overall product name is the unique master name of the overall product 
| Specific Product Name | administrative.specific-product.name | Recommended | The Specific product name is the unique master name of the specific product within the company
| Simulation task confidentiality level | administrative.simulation-task.confidentiality | Recommended | The Simulation task confidentiality level is a  classification with respect to the access to contained information.
| Predicted simulation start date | administrative.simulation-task.date.start | Recommended | The Predicted simulation start date is the date at which all information will have been provided so that the simulation service can start.
| Requested simulation delivery date | administrative.simulation-task.date.delivery | Recommended | The Requested simulation delivery date is the due date that the simulation results will be appreciated by the ordering person or organization. 
| Requesting person | administrative.contact.customer-person | Recommended | The Requesting person is the person who needs the obtained simulation results 
| Requesting entity | administrative.contact.customer-entity | Recommended | The Requesting entity is the department or external company that needs the obtained simulation results 
| Contracting person | administrative.contact.contractor-person | Recommended | The Contracting person is the person who accepts the simulation request to provide a simulation service.
| Contracting entity | administrative.contact.contractor-entity | Recommended | The Contracting entity is the department or external company who accepts the simulation request to provide a simulation service.
| Simulation request creator name | administrative.simulation-request.contact.insert-name | Optional | The Simulation request creator name is the name or other regulatory-compliant valid identifier of the person who initially created the simulation request
| Simulation request creation date | administrative.simulation-request.date.submitted-system | Optional | The Simulation request  creation date is the date when the simulation request in the project management IT system was created
| Simulation task creator name | administrative.simulation-task.contact.insert-name | Optional | The Simulation task creator name is the name or other regulatory-compliant valid identifier of the person who initially created the simulation task
| Simulation task creation date | administrative.simulation-task.date.creation | Optional | The Simulation request creation date is the date the simulation request in the project management IT system was created.
|===

==== Analysis

[cols="1>m,2^e,7<",width="70%",align="center",frame="topbot",options="header"]
|===
| MIC Sim. Task Name| STMD Mapping | Obligation | Short Explanation
| Name of Item under test | analysis.item-under-test.name | Mandatory | The Item under test specifies the product, system, component, or software, etc.  that is subject of a simulation being requested
| Simulation objective | analysis.simulation-task.objectives | Mandatory | The Simulation objective represents the engineering question that is to be answered by the simulation task. 
| Simulation further requirement | analysis.simulation-task.further-requirements | Mandatory | The Simulation further requirement describes what is to be determined, confirmed, or neglected by a simulation.
| Quality criterion | analysis.quality-aspects | Mandatory | A Quality criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation, particularly given the importance of the decision to be made.
| Higher level engineering task | analysis.engineering-task.higher-level | Recommended | The Higher level engineering task is a higher level engineering task that sets the context of the specific engineering task.
| Specific engineering task | analysis.engineering-task.specific | Recommended | The Specific engineering task is the engineering that directly benefits from the simulation results.
| Verification criterion | analysis.verification | Recommended | A Verification criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation in order to verify that the simulation results align with the specified objectives of the simulation task
| Validation criterion | analysis.validation | Recommended | A Validation criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation in order to validate that the simulation results align with the needs of the specific engineering task that requested the simulation.
| Analysis verification result  | analysis.verification | Recommended | The Analysis verification result is the conclusion of the analysis phase closing verification.
|===
