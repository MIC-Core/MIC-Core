==== Analysis

[cols="1>m,2^e,7<",width="70%",align="center",frame="topbot",options="header"]
|===
| MIC Sim. Task Name| STMD Mapping | Obligation | Short Explanation
| Name of Item under test | analysis.item-under-test.name | #Mandatory# | The Item under test specifies the product, system, component, or software, etc.  that is subject of a simulation being requested
| Simulation objective | analysis.simulation-task.objectives | Mandatory | The Simulation objective represents the engineering question that is to be answered by the simulation task. 
| Simulation further requirement | analysis.simulation-task.further-requirements | Mandatory | The Simulation further requirement describes what is to be determined, confirmed, or neglected by a simulation.
| Quality criterion | analysis.quality-aspects | #Mandatory# | A Quality criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation, particularly given the importance of the decision to be made.
| Higher level engineering task | analysis.engineering-task.higher-level | Recommended | The Higher level engineering task is a higher level engineering task that sets the context of the specific engineering task.
| Specific engineering task | analysis.engineering-task.specific | Recommended | The Specific engineering task is the engineering that directly benefits from the simulation results.
| Verification criterion | analysis.verification | Recommended | A Verification criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation in order to verify that the simulation results align with the specified objectives of the simulation task
| Validation criterion | analysis.validation | Recommended | A Validation criterion is a quality requirement that must be met during the simulation in order to validate that the simulation results align with the needs of the specific engineering task that requested the simulation.
| Analysis verification result  | analysis.verification | Recommended | The Analysis verification result is the conclusion of the analysis phase closing verification.
|===
